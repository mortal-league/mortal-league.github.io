Guild Reputation

Reputation rewards verified public guild activity. It remains separate from Territory and War. One event receives one award.

Connection statusRules and draft builder: openProof upload: not connectedProvisional ladder: not connectedObjections and referee queue: not connected

Activity categories

Patrol

Organized area security or presence.

Roam

Structured movement seeking interaction.

Training

Planned instruction or practice.

Scrim

Arranged practice against another group.

Raffle or giveaway

A free public distribution event.

Trade

A public market or organized exchange.

Supply

A documented supply run or delivery.

Escort

Protection provided during travel.

Recruitment

A public guild recruitment event.

Community

A service or event for players beyond the guild.

Diplomatic

A documented agreement between guilds.

Miscellaneous

Other proven public guild activity.
