How scoring works

  1. 01

    Join

    After service launch, register a guild, apply through Guild Finder, or accept a private invitation. The guild enters both MO2 ladders.

  2. 02

    Play

    Teams complete approved goals in the game.

  3. 03

    Report

    Any listed member sends the action to the correct ladder.

  4. 04

    Review

    Neutral refs check the proof and publish a ruling.

  5. 05

    Finish

    Final rulings lock scores and name each champion.
