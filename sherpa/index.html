Role-specific League Standing

Participant

Event conduct, accurate reports, deadlines, and compliance with final rulings.

Sherpa

Participant standing plus service time, useful feedback, training record, and learner safety.

Referee

Participant standing plus conflict disclosure, recusal, proof handling, ruling quality, and appeal conduct.

Referee pipeline
Experienced Sherpas may apply separately for referee work after meeting service, time, feedback, conduct, and standing requirements. Referees must disclose conflicts and recuse from their own guild’s cases. Sherpa service alone does not appoint a referee.
