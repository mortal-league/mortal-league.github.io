Reading the labels

Four kinds of claim.

Every newsletter item carries a label so evidence and judgment do not blur together.

Verified fact

A source records it.

The issue links the source, date, platform, measurement window, and important limits.

Announced plan

A responsible party said it.

The statement remains a plan until a later source confirms that it shipped or happened.

Interpretation

The evidence supports a reading.

The issue names the inference and keeps it separate from the underlying facts.

Unknown

The public record does not settle it.

The issue states the missing evidence instead of converting rumor into a conclusion.
