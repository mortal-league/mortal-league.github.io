The numbers need context.

A point-in-time player count, a promotion, a review score, and a development announcement describe different parts of Mortal Online 2. Treating them as one answer destroys the information each source actually contains.

One number is not “the population”

Concurrent Steam players, purchasers, subscribers, unique players, returning players, guild activity, and continent-specific participation answer different questions. The League should publish the narrow number it has and refuse the larger claim it cannot support.

Official development record

Era of Refinement

Announced PTR changes matter to organized play, but an announcement is not a live rule state.

Announced plan

Patch 1 was announced for PTR testing on October 14.

Star Vault’s October 6 development update described proposed combat, quality-of-life, gathering, crafting, heraldry, loot, event, and clade changes. The post said the values and included changes remained subject to feedback, testing, alteration, or removal.

Items with direct league consequences include the parry and stamina rework, lower adrenaline gain from taking damage, stronger minimum weapon charge, visible debuffs, bandage timing changes, visible guild heraldry, and planned high-activity days for relic drops and dynamic events.

Direct source: Into the Vault #162
Interpretation

The League should freeze a ruleset against a named live patch.

A PTR build should not silently alter an active match record. Every declaration, ruling, standing change, and season should name the live game version used. Material combat changes require a posted effective date or the next season boundary.

Measurement controls

Incident windows

An outage or attack may suppress concurrency, distort playtime, produce review bursts, and prevent scheduled events.

Unknown

No direct official incident record was located for the reported DDoS window.

Player reports alone do not establish the start, end, scope, cause, or affected services. This issue therefore does not convert the reported disruption into a verified Star Vault incident.

Future weekly comparisons should mark confirmed DDoS, outage, maintenance, queue, login, and PTR periods. If a disruption overlaps a League event, the referee record should pause settlement until both sides receive the same opportunity to submit evidence.

Access question

Free Haven

A free Haven entry path could change recruitment and onboarding, but the current public record does not support a launch claim.

Unknown

No current official launch date or operative Free Haven offer was located.

An archived May 2022 forum summary reported a plan to make Haven a free-to-play area in the future. That old statement does not establish present scope, timing, account limits, chat access, character transfer, or conversion rules.

Historical source: May 2022 stream summary
Interpretation

The League should prepare onboarding without advertising an unannounced product.

Guild Finder, Sherpa service, build planning, and beginner events remain useful whether new players arrive through a sale, referral, free trial, or a future Haven program. The tools should describe current access truthfully and update when Star Vault publishes operative terms.

What the week changes

League consequences

The newsletter exists to turn public signals into bounded operational decisions.

  1. InterpretationLower coordination friction.A modest visible Steam population increases the value of Guild Finder, Sherpa service, scheduled declarations, and continent-specific ladders. The League should make finding organized play easier than finding it through private chat alone.
  2. InterpretationPrepare for sale traffic.The current promotion gives guilds a recruitment window. Recruitment listings should state time zone, activity schedule, continent, new-player policy, and application requirements before newcomers arrive.
  3. InterpretationName the patch.Scrims, sieges, objectives, evidence rules, and rulings should record the live version. Announced PTR balance does not enter League scoring until it reaches live service and receives an effective date.
  4. InterpretationProtect disrupted events.Confirmed DDoS, outage, maintenance, and login incidents need a neutral incident window. Referees should reschedule or suspend settlement instead of guessing which side would have won.
