    Safe play
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Account safety
    
            
      
              
    • Use a different password for email, Discord, and Steam. Turn on two-step login.
      • 
              
    • Remove old logins and recovery methods you do not control.
      • 
              
    • Keep legal names, addresses, phone numbers, and family details out of public profiles.
      • 
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Network safety
    
            
      
              
    • Do not share IP addresses, router screenshots, or provider account details.
      • 
              
    • Call your internet provider during an attack. Save the ticket number.
      • 
              
    • Do not retaliate, scan another player, or try to identify them yourself.
      • 
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Evidence safety
    
            
      
              
    • Save original files, links, dates, times, time zones, and account IDs.
      • 
              
    • Keep one clean copy before you mark, crop, or hide anything.
      • 
              
    • Do not repost threats, private images, or personal details.
