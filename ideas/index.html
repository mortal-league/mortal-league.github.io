Moderation and scope

The Idea Parking Lot is a community board, not an official Star Vault support channel. Approval here does not mean Star Vault has seen, accepted, or promised to act on a post. Account problems, cheating reports, security issues, harassment, and private evidence belong in the appropriate official or private reporting route. Site moderators may merge duplicates, edit labels, return unclear submissions, or remove unsafe content.
