League titles

Highest final Territory score.

War Champion

Reputation Champion

Highest final Guild Reputation score.

A title needs at least two guilds with approved scores. There is no combined champion.

Report and review process

  1. Submit.Add one clear screenshot and explain the activity. More images are optional.
  2. Check.The system checks the date, proof, details, and duplicate files.
  3. Post.A clear Reputation ticket gets provisional points.
  4. Object.Other participating guilds get 48 hours to object.
  5. Review.An officer from another guild decides a disputed ticket.
  6. Record.The final ruling enters the Integrity Registry.

Private guild records

Private member points never enter the League by themselves. A guild member must choose to send a separate League report.
