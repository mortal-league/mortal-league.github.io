# Mortal League Beta

Public rules, planning tools, printable downloads, and league reference pages for Mortal Online 1 and Mortal Online 2.

Community pages include Guild Finder, Guild Reputation, Sherpa and League Standing, Exploits Info, the Idea Parking Lot, Mortal League Weekly, and the closed-during-Beta wager form. Guild registrations, player applications, and League reports may be prepared as private local drafts. The static site does not send them.

Mortal League Weekly is a permanent, source-linked archive covering population signals, development updates, incidents, promotions, and practical league consequences. Reports label claims as Verified fact, Announced plan, Interpretation, or Unknown. The site provides a public RSS feed, but email and Discord delivery are not connected.

The declaration builder prepares Siege, Scrim, and Objective drafts with gold and Standing offers. Base Standing is capped at 500 points. Optional Standing Double Jeopardy adds another copy of the declared Standing stake for each eligible friendly death. The builder remains local-only and does not accept, escrow, or settle wagers during Beta.

The Idea Parking Lot uses public GitHub issues as its submission and moderation queue. Site moderators add the `idea-lot:approved` label to publish a post on the grid. The grid counts thumbs-up reactions as likes and sorts approved posts by likes, comments, and publication date. See `.github/IDEA_LOT_MODERATION.md` for setup and review steps.

This is an independent community project. Star Vault does not operate or approve it.

Guild registration and shared scoring remain closed until the private data service moves to a neutral host and passes its launch checks.
