# Mortal League Beta

Public rules, planning tools, printable downloads, and league reference pages for Mortal Online 1 and Mortal Online 2.

Community pages include Guild Finder, Guild Reputation, Sherpa and League Standing, Exploits Info, the Idea Parking Lot, Mortal League Weekly, and the Event Declaration builder. Guild registrations, player applications, declarations, and League reports may be prepared as private local drafts. The static site does not send them.

Mortal League Weekly is a permanent, source-linked archive covering population signals, development updates, incidents, promotions, and practical league consequences. Reports label claims as Verified fact, Announced plan, Interpretation, or Unknown. The site provides a public RSS feed, but email and Discord delivery are not connected.

The About page contains the official MO League Statement on Player Organizing. The statement uses 17 historical records from 2008 through 2026 to explain how voluntary player institutions support Mortal Online's stated emphasis on interaction, interdependence, reputation, trade, territory, and player-created activity. It recognizes earlier guild, forum, Discord, tournament, and community-event organizing and claims no Star Vault endorsement.

The declaration builder prepares local Siege, Scrim, and Objective drafts with proposed teams, timing, victory conditions, proof, and cancellation terms. Gold stakes, Standing stakes, middlers, custody, settlement, and Standing Double Jeopardy remain disabled throughout Beta.

Exploits Info publishes no classifications until a description, status, source, effective date, and authority boundary are confirmed. The local report builder does not upload selected screenshots or send a report.

The Idea Parking Lot uses public GitHub issues as its submission and moderation queue. Site moderators add the `idea-lot:approved` label to publish a post on the grid. The grid counts thumbs-up reactions as likes and sorts approved posts by likes, comments, and publication date. See `.github/IDEA_LOT_MODERATION.md` for setup and review steps.

This is an independent community project. Star Vault does not operate or approve it.

Guild registration and shared scoring remain closed until the private data service moves to a neutral host and passes its launch checks.
