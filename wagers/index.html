Middlers and referees
A middler may hold and transfer agreed stakes without accepting referee duties. A referee who also serves as middler must meet both role standards and disclose the combined role. First, second, and third choices identify three different people. No person becomes a middler or referee merely because a draft names them.

    


    
      
Optional wager term
Standing Double Jeopardy

      
Double Jeopardy adds one extra copy of a side’s declared Standing stake for every eligible friendly death that side suffers during the accepted event.

      
ExampleBase Standing stake: 100Eligible friendly deaths: 3Total Standing exposure: 100 + (100 × 3) = 400

      
The 500-point limit applies to the base Standing stake. Double Jeopardy may raise final exposure above 500. The accepted event record must define an eligible death, the scoring window, duplicate-death limits, exclusions, and the proof standard before the wager is locked.
